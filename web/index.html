  1. Sign in with Discord

    Your account keeps your cards and coins. If this browser has an older save, you can transfer it after you sign in.

    Start in the Pack shop and open a Common pack to get your first fighters.

  3. Build your squad

    Take four cards into the campaign. Use Help in each section to learn what to do there.
